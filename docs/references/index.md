# Reference Index

This is the canonical catalog of versioned evidence and working material used
to define the Cloud SPE's portion of the Network Engineering SPE Build Track.
Entries are ordered by the date of the event, response, observation, or artifact,
with the newest first. Preparation dates are described in the context when they
differ from the date represented by the artifact.

Files in this index are not automatically normative. Meeting participation does
not itself establish decision authority, survey responses are individual input,
and working analyses remain proposals until accepted through the appropriate
Cloud SPE and Network Engineering SPE process. Deployed behavior, accepted
decisions, product specifications, and design documents retain the precedence
defined in [repository architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#source-of-truth-hierarchy).

Ignored raw transcripts and automated Gemini note files are generally not
versioned or linked. The explicitly supplied 23 and 24 September text transcripts are
exceptions and are indexed below. Their relevant findings and evidence limitations are
preserved in the dated source summaries and consolidated meeting records below.

## Maintenance rule

When adding or materially changing a reference:

1. place it in the directory matching its functional role;
2. give it an explicit event, response, observation, or preparation date;
3. record participants or respondent identity and role where applicable;
4. state provenance and evidence limitations;
5. add or update exactly one chronological-catalog row;
6. preserve the distinction between evidence, recommendation, working
   alignment, and accepted decision;
7. verify every local link with `python3 scripts/check_docs.py`; and
8. record work state, unresolved dependencies, and handoffs in Beads rather
   than in a competing Markdown task list.

If a reference becomes normative, retain its dated reference entry for
provenance and link to the accepted decision, design document, or product
specification that supersedes it.
